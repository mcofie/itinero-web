# Travel album cover artwork

Generated with the built-in image generation tool from the three user-supplied October 10 reference images. The Italy output was then used as the shared style reference for all five companion covers. Originals are 1024 × 1536 PNGs and are served without image recompression.

Assets: `public/images/landing/covers/{italy,istanbul,rome,vietnam,tokyo,amsterdam}.png`.

## Prompts

### italy

Use case: illustration-story. Asset: production artwork for a travel photo album cover, portrait 2:3, highest available resolution and crisp clean print-quality edges.
Use the uploaded book photos and poster grid as STYLE REFERENCES. Recreate the visual language of the ITALY lemon cover in reference 1 faithfully: mustard golden yellow background, enormous heavy condensed uppercase pale butter-yellow ITALY across the top, small 2025 aligned right directly below. Main illustration in the lower two thirds is two beautifully drawn overlapping lemon cross-sections with dark olive green leaves. Match the confident mid-century gouache/screenprinted travel-poster forms in the reference, sophisticated organic silhouette edges, restrained 5-color palette, clear seed and segment details. Flat opaque colors with only extremely subtle paper character, no grain filter or noisy texture, no gradient. Strong professional illustration, NOT crude geometric clipart. 
Output ONLY the flat rectangular printed cover art filling the entire image edge to edge. No book mockup, no spine, no shadow, no frame, no perspective, no rotation, no backdrop, no margins outside the artwork, no extra text or watermark. Typesetting and illustration must be sharp. All future covers in this series will share this exact typography, title baseline, year alignment, illustration scale and composition. Text verbatim: ITALY and 2025.
